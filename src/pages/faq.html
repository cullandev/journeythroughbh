FAQ

Common questions

If yours isn't here, ask it during the free consultation. There are no silly ones.

How do I start?

Send a message through the contact page or call. I'll get back to you to set up a free 15-minute consultation. After that, if we both think it's a good fit, we schedule a first session. The full process is here.

Is what I say confidential?

Yes. What you share in therapy stays between us, and I won't confirm to anyone that you're a client without your written permission. There are a small number of legal exceptions, which I'll go over with you in the first session and which are spelled out in the Notice of Privacy Practices. In short, they involve serious risk of harm to you or someone else, suspected abuse of a child or vulnerable adult, or a court order.

How does telehealth work?

You need a private space, a device with a camera, and a decent connection. Telehealth works well for most concerns; if I think in-person care would serve you better, I'll say so.

How long are sessions, and how often do we meet?

Sessions are TODO: 50 minutes. Most people begin weekly, which builds momentum, and then move to every other week as things settle. There's no required number of sessions.

What does it cost, and do you take insurance?

Rates, insurance, and superbill details are on the fees and insurance page, along with the required Good Faith Estimate notice.

What happens in the first session?

Mostly talking. I'll ask about what's bringing you in, what's going on in your life right now, and what you'd like to be different. You can share as much or as little as feels right. We'll also cover practical things like scheduling. People often leave the first session feeling relieved that it wasn't a big deal.

What if we're not a good fit?

That happens, and it's not a failure on anyone's part. Say so, and I'll help you find someone who might suit you better. The free consultation exists partly to catch this early.

Can you prescribe medication?

No. As a TODO: license type I provide talk therapy, not prescriptions. If medication might help, we can discuss it and I can coordinate with your doctor or a psychiatric prescriber, with your permission.

What if I'm in crisis?

This practice isn't set up for emergencies, and I'm not available around the clock. If you're in immediate danger, call 911. If you're thinking about suicide or in emotional crisis, call or text 988 any time. More crisis resources are here.

Does this website track me?

No. There are no analytics scripts, advertising pixels, social media widgets, or third-party fonts, and no cookies are set. The only outside service is Cloudflare's bot check on the contact form. Details are in the privacy policy.
