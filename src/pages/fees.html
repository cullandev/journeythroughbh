The insurance plans I accept, and self-pay, before you ever have to ask.

Insurance

I am in network with:

  • Aetna
  • Anthem Blue Cross Blue Shield
  • Optum
  • United Healthcare

Your copay or coinsurance depends on your plan. It helps to call the number on your card before the first session and ask about your outpatient mental health benefits, so you know what to expect.

Self-pay is also welcome if you don't have one of these plans, or would rather not use your insurance.

Some people choose to pay privately even when they have coverage, because it keeps a diagnosis off their insurance record. We can talk through the trade-offs.
