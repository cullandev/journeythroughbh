What to expect

From first contact to first session

Here is exactly what happens, in order. Most people are surprised by how ordinary it is.

  1. You reach out

    Use the contact form, call, or email. All you need to give is your name and a way to reach you. Please don't include health details; we'll cover those privately later.

  2. I reply

    Usually within TODO: e.g. one business day, by the method you chose. If you leave a voicemail, say whether it's okay to leave a message when I call back.

  3. Free 15-minute consultation

    A short phone or video call. I'll ask what's bringing you in and what you're hoping for. You can ask me anything: how I work, fees, scheduling, whether I've helped people with similar things. At the end we decide together whether to book a first session. If I'm not the right fit, I'll say so and try to point you somewhere better.

  4. Paperwork, online

    Before the first session you'll get a link to the client portal (TODO: SimplePractice / TherapyNotes / other) to complete intake forms, consent, and the privacy notice. That's where health information belongs, not on this website.

  5. First session

    Mostly conversation. I'll want to understand what's going on now, some of your history, and what "better" would look like for you. You don't have to tell me everything on day one. We'll also sketch out how often to meet.

  6. Ongoing sessions

    We work on what you came in for, and we check in regularly about whether it's helping. Therapy ends when you decide it has done its job. There is no minimum commitment.
