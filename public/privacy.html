What this site collects

Almost nothing. Specifically:

  • No cookies. The site sets none, so there is no cookie banner.
  • No analytics or advertising trackers. No Google Analytics, no Meta Pixel, no social media buttons or embeds.
  • No third-party fonts. Fonts are served from this site.
  • No embedded maps. The map link opens a separate site in a new request that you choose to make.

The contact form

If you use the form, we receive only what you type: your name, your preferred contact method, an email address or phone number, and an optional short message. It is delivered to us by email and used solely to reply to you. The form asks you not to include health details, and we don't add what you write to any health record.

To block automated spam, the form uses Cloudflare Turnstile, which runs a check in your browser when the page loads. Cloudflare may process technical information such as your IP address and browser characteristics to do so. See Cloudflare's privacy policy. Turnstile does not build advertising profiles.

Hosting and server logs

The site is hosted on Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes IP addresses and request details to deliver pages and protect against abuse. We do not use this information to identify visitors. TODO: if you enable Cloudflare Web Analytics (cookieless), mention it here.

Your choices
